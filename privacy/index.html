Privacy Policy

Undertone · Last updated November 7, 2026

Undertone helps you understand conversations and decide what to say. It is built so that what you write and import stays on your phone. This policy explains what leaves your device, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Undertone is operated by Heitor Kunrath ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: heitorkunrath@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your people, notes, analyses, chat messages with the Wise Friend, drafts and program progress are stored only on your phone, encrypted with AES-256 where the device supports secure storage.
  • Imported chats are never saved in full, on your phone or on our servers. Only derived results (such as statistics and the answer you got) are kept, on your phone.
  • You can delete all of it at any time: Me → Delete all my data.

What we process to give you an answer

When you ask for an analysis, the text, screenshots, chat excerpts or audio you choose to send, plus the notes about that person, are sent to our servers and passed to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the answer. Voice clips are first turned into text by a speech-to-text provider. We do not store this content on our servers, we do not log it, and it is not used to train AI models. It is processed only to answer your request.

What we store on our servers

Never the content of your conversations. We store:

  • Your account: your email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier they give us.
  • Usage counters: how many times you used each feature, to apply the free and paid limits.
  • Referrals: your invite code, and who redeemed whose code and when (identifiers and dates only).
  • Purchases: your plan, its status and history (product, amount, currency, dates). Payments are handled by Google Play, the App Store or Stripe; we never see your card details. When a subscription ends we note the date, so we can tell whether you are a former subscriber.
  • App usage events: which screens and features are used and how often, linked to your account (or to a random install ID before you sign in). These events contain names of features and counts, never what you wrote or imported.

To keep your plan working if our payment provider is briefly unavailable, our servers keep your account ID and your current plan (including any Deep Report credits) in temporary memory for up to 72 hours after we last confirmed it. This is never written to a database and is erased when the server restarts. The app also keeps your account ID and last confirmed plan, with the time it was confirmed, on your device for up to 72 hours for the same reason. Neither includes anything you wrote or imported.

Emails

We email you sign-in and password-reset codes. If you start a free trial on the web, we email you before it ends (7 and 2 days before) with the date, the amount and how to cancel. These emails say nothing about how you use the app.

Invites

When you open an Undertone invite link, our hosting provider logs the request (including your IP address, browser type and the invite code in the link) for up to 30 days, to count how many people open invites and to prevent abuse. When you install the app from an invite, the app reads the invite code and campaign tags from Google Play or from the link on first launch, so your friend's code can be applied. We never read your clipboard.

Service providers

We use these providers only to run the service, under their data-processing terms:

  • Amazon Web Services (hosting, database, email delivery), United States
  • Anthropic (AI answers), United States
  • A speech-to-text provider (voice clips only)
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple (in-app purchases)
  • Stripe (purchases on the web)
  • Google and Apple (only if you choose to sign in with them)

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Other people in your conversations

Conversations you import include messages from other people. They are processed only on your instruction, to answer your request, and are not stored on our servers. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing.

How long we keep data

Account, purchase, counter and referral data are kept while you have an account. Invite-link logs are deleted after 30 days. When you delete your account, everything we store about you is deleted, except records we must keep by law (such as tax records of payments, which do not include app content).

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Me → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your account and server data and erases the app's data on that device. Without the app, email us from your account's address and we will delete it.
  • Access or correct your data: email us. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or Brazil), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to processing. We honor these requests for everyone.
  • Cancel a subscription in your Google Play or App Store account, or in the web Customer Portal. Deleting the app does not cancel a store subscription.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On your phone it is encrypted at rest where supported, and you can turn on an app lock. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.

Children

Undertone is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
