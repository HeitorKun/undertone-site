Delete your Okayso account

In the app

  1. Open Okayso and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email heitorkunrath@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Okayso account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, password hash, Apple or Google sign-in identifiers).
  • Your usage counters, referral codes and referral records, purchase records we mirror, and app usage events linked to your account.
  • Deleting in the app also erases everything stored on that device (people, notes, analyses, chats, drafts).

What may be kept

Records we must keep by law, such as tax records of payments, which never include app content. Invite-link access logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in your store account first.
